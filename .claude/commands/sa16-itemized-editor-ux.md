Step 0 (plan, wait for approval, with an ASCII layout of the new editor at 5 and 15 members): (1) state: `ItemDraft.quantity`; `ItemizedDraft` discount (on/mode/text), `ChargeMode {percent, amount}` for discount, service and tax, `adjustmentAuto`; controller methods (`setItemQuantity`, `setDiscount*`, `setChargeMode`, `setAllAssignees`, `toggleAssigneeForPerson`, duplicate item, restore a deleted item for undo); `buildItemizedCommand` and the preview builder send the new fields (SA15 contract). (2) Assignment modes: segmented "حسب الصنف | حسب الشخص"; by item = cards; by person = a person dropdown (avatar, name, running total from the preview) plus a checklist of all items (name, ×qty × price, checkbox); an item nobody has is flagged in both modes. (3) Compact item card, about two rows: name, quantity stepper, unit price, ⋮ menu (Duplicate, Delete with an undo snackbar); then an assignee summary chip (up to 3 avatars, "+N" or "الكل") with the line total; groups of 5 or fewer members keep inline chips. (4) `MemberPickerSheet`: bottom sheet, search shown above 8 members, all/none toggle, 48dp rows, sticky "تم (n)" button, reused by the person dropdown and, where it fits, by `PayersSheet`. (5) Charges card: discount row above service and tax; each row has a switch, a `% | ج.م` toggle, a value box and the preview amount; the tax-base chips only when both service and tax are on. (6) Printed-total tolerance (A174, 18.3): after a preview, with a printed total, an unedited adjustment and a difference of 100 piasters or less, set the adjustment and preview again, show "تم ضبط فرق X قرش تلقائيًا لمطابقة إجمالي الإيصال"; any manual edit clears the auto flag; if the engine rejects it, keep the mismatch banner; the constant `kPrintedTotalTolerance = 100` lives in one place. (7) Breakdown card: one row per person that expands to items, discount, service and tax. (8) Validation: required marks and Arabic labels; Save stays enabled; tapping it with errors marks every item touched, scrolls to the first error (one key per section), and shows a count in the bottom bar; errors have an icon and text, never colour alone. (9) CTA pass: one primary sticky "حفظ الفاتورة" with a loading state; "+ إضافة صنف" as an outlined full-width button; "مسح الإيصال" tonal with icon and label; destructive actions only in the ⋮ menu or with a confirm; all targets ≥ 48dp; icon-only buttons have `Semantics`. (10) Read-only details (P7-01): `×qty`, discount line, amount-form charges, assignees as "names +N" that expand. (11) New Arabic copy and ARB strings (ux-copy), and the scope guard additions.
